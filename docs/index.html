TRY TO FOOL IT

Write the best lie you can.

The protocol below is live on GenLayer right now and it is not breaking its line. Send the validators anything you like about it. If you can talk them into stopping it, you have broken this, and the attempt is on chain either way.

A real transaction and a real round, about a minute. Connect a wallet on GenLayer Studionet and put 0.002 GEN behind what you write. That deposit is the only thing between this and a button anybody can press to stop a competitor, so the demo does not waive it.

    Limits
    
            
    What it will not do.
    
        
    

        
    
            
    
                
    It does not find exploits.
    
                
    
                    Somebody has to see it and say so. What this removes is the wait between the seeing and the stopping.
                
    
            
    
            
    
                
    It judges what is, never what might be.
    
                
    
                    A theoretical bug or a risk with no event behind it is refused at the cost of the deposit.
                
    
            
    
            
    
                
    It does not stop an atomic exploit.
    
                
    
                    Nothing outside the transaction can. This is built for drains that take minutes and hours.
