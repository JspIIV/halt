The stop rests on a reading, not on a fact. Those are two independent depositors who happen to hold similar positions in a vault that had two users at the time, and calling them one actor is speculation about intent that nothing in our ledger supports. Similar amounts and nearby timestamps are what a small vault looks like: with two users, any two deposits are necessarily close together and in some order, and any pattern at all can be described as lockstep after the fact. Neither address withdrew more than forty percent of its own position, so neither crossed any limit that applies to an address. We ask that the stop be lifted and the burden put back on whoever wants to prove these two parties are the same one.
