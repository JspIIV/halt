The line our vault published forbids an address withdrawing more than half of its deposit inside a ten minute window. Read as written, that is a statement about a withdrawal, and no withdrawal here came close: there were three of them and each was a quarter of the position, comfortably inside the limit on every occasion. The alarm has added three lawful actions together and called the sum unlawful, which is not what the line says and not what a depositor reading it would understand. If we had meant to cap the total we would have written the total. The stop should be lifted.
