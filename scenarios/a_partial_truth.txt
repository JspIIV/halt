We can account for both of those addresses and there is no coordination here. One of them is our own treasury wallet, which we use to seed liquidity in a new vault so that the first outside depositor is not alone in the pool, and the other belongs to a customer who deposited shortly afterwards and later withdrew for reasons of their own. The similarity in size and timing is a consequence of how the vault was launched, not evidence of a single actor. We accept that the pattern looks like the one the line describes and we will change how we seed vaults in future, but a treasury seeding its own pool is not the abuse the line was written to stop, and the stop should be lifted.
